Privacy Policy – DuetTalk

Last updated: October 2026

DuetTalk is a private, on-device conversation helper. Two people share one screen. Saved phrases, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload what you type.

Data stored on device

  • Saved phrases, and a chat only when you leave “Delete the chat when you finish” off
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and DuetTalk Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and DuetTalk Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N305.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
