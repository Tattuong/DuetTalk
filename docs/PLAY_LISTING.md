## Short description

## Full description
DuetTalk is for noisy rooms, hard-of-hearing conversations, and any moment it is easier to type than to talk. Split the screen into two halves facing opposite ways, or pass the phone and take turns.

Type a line, hand the phone over, and open the last sentence large. Save quick replies. A privacy switch deletes the chat when you finish. Saved phrases stay on the phone. No account.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and DuetTalk Pro (more than 8 saved phrases) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and DuetTalk Pro are one-time unlocks.
